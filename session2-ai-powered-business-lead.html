Claude is an AI assistant built by Anthropic. You talk to it in plain English — and it writes, analyses, summarises, and creates for you.

Three things to remember from Session 1:

💬

It's a Conversation

You type, it responds. The clearer you communicate, the better the result. No code needed.

Confident, Not Infallible

If you can write an email, you can use Claude. Today we go from "using it" to "leading with it."

The Framework
R – C – T: Three Letters, Better Results
R

Role

Who should Claude act as? — "You are a Brand Strategist," "Act as a senior HR Business Partner," "You are a financial analyst."

C

Context

What background does Claude need? — "We are launching a new Deltek Vantagepoint feature," "This NDA is for a mid-market SaaS deal," "Q3 revenue is down 8%."

T

Task

What exactly should Claude deliver? — "Write three social media hooks targeting Project Accountants," "Flag the 3 most aggressive indemnity clauses," "Build a comparison table."

Role sets the expertise. Context sets the scene. Task sets the deliverable.

Nail all three and you get first-draft quality that's ready to edit — not rewrite.

R-C-T in Action
See the Difference

Vague Delegation

Prompt "Write something about our new feature for social media."

Result: generic filler that sounds like every other LinkedIn post.

R-C-T Delegation

Prompt "You are a Brand Strategist (Role). We are launching a new Deltek Vantagepoint feature for resource planning (Context). Write three social media hooks targeting Project Accountants — each under 150 characters, with a clear call-to-action (Task)."

Result: three sharp, audience-specific hooks ready for review.

More domain examples:

Legal / Compliance

Prompt "Review this NDA (Context). Flag the 3 most aggressive indemnity clauses (Task) and explain them like I'm a Sales Rep (Persona layer)."

HR / Talent

Prompt "You are a senior HR Business Partner (Role). An employee received mixed feedback in their annual review (Context). Draft a 90-day development plan focused on leadership growth (Task)."

Ground Rules
The Rules Haven't Changed

Do

  1. Use R-C-T every time — Role, Context, Task
  2. Iterate — refine the output instead of starting over
  3. Ask Claude to explain its reasoning when something looks off
  4. Start with low-stakes tasks to build confidence
  5. Use Artifacts for anything visual — charts, tables, dashboards
  6. Share your best prompts with your team

Don't

  1. Paste confidential data without checking your org's AI policy
  2. Trust numbers or claims without independent verification
  3. Send AI-generated output without reading it first
  4. Give up after one bad result — refine your prompt
  5. Skip the human-in-the-loop step — you own the final call
  6. Overthink it — the best way to learn is to start
Claude is confident, not always correct. Your judgment is still the most important ingredient.

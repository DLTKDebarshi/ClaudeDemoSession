DELTEK Q3 2026 BUDGET CONSTRAINTS MEMO
Confidential | Finance Department | Issued April 1, 2026

TO: Department Heads
FROM: CFO Office
RE: Q3 2026 New Vendor Spend Guidelines

1. TOTAL NEW VENDOR SPEND CAP
   - Q3 2026 cap: $150,000 for all new vendor commitments combined.
   - This applies to net-new vendors only. Renewals of existing contracts are budgeted separately.

2. CURRENT COMMITMENTS AGAINST CAP
   - Snowflake expansion (Data Science): $72,000
   - Figma Enterprise upgrade (Design): $40,000
   - Total committed: $112,000
   - REMAINING HEADROOM: $38,000

3. APPROVAL THRESHOLDS
   - Under $10,000: Department VP approval.
   - $10,000 - $25,000: Department VP + Finance Director approval.
   - Over $25,000: CFO approval required.

4. CONTRACT STRUCTURE PREFERENCES
   - Annual contracts with quarterly payment terms preferred.
   - Monthly contracts acceptable for pilot/evaluation periods (max 3 months).
   - Multi-year commitments require Board approval — no exceptions.

5. VENDOR CONSOLIDATION INCENTIVE
   - Cost savings achieved through vendor consolidation are EXEMPT from the Q3 cap.
   - Example: Replacing two $15K tools with one $20K tool counts as a $10K savings, not a $20K spend.

6. EXCEPTIONS PROCESS
   - All exceptions require written justification submitted to the CFO office.
   - Turnaround time: 5 business days.
   - Emergency exceptions (business-critical): 48-hour turnaround via CFO direct approval.

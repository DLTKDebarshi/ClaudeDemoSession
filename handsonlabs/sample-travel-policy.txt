DELTEK CORPORATE TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY
Version 3.2 | Effective January 2026 | HR Department

1. AIRFARE
   - Domestic: Economy class only. Flights over $800 require pre-approval from department manager.
   - International: Business class permitted for flights exceeding 6 hours duration.
   - All bookings must be made through the approved travel portal (Concur).

2. HOTEL
   - Tier 1 Cities (NYC, SF, LA, DC, Chicago, Boston, Seattle): Maximum $225/night.
   - All Other Cities: Maximum $175/night.
   - Extended stays (5+ nights): Contact Procurement for negotiated rates.

3. MEALS & INCIDENTALS
   - Domestic: $75/day maximum.
   - International: $100/day maximum.
   - Alcohol is not reimbursable.

4. VENDOR-HOSTED TRAVEL
   - All vendor-hosted travel must be pre-approved by department VP.
   - Vendors may NOT pay for airfare directly. Reimbursement model only.
   - Vendor-hosted meals at conferences are exempt from daily meal caps.

5. GIFTS FROM VENDORS
   - Maximum value: $50 per gift.
   - Gifts exceeding $50 in value require disclosure to the Ethics Committee within 5 business days.
   - Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (gift cards) are prohibited regardless of value.

6. EXPENSE SUBMISSION
   - All expenses must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the trip.
   - Receipts required for any expense over $25.
   - Late submissions (30-60 days) require manager approval. Submissions over 60 days will not be reimbursed.

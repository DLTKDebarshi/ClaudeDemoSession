DELTEK STANDARD VENDOR AGREEMENT TERMS
2025 Template | Legal Department

1. PAYMENT TERMS
   - Net 45 from date of invoice.
   - Quarterly billing preferred for annual contracts.

2. LIABILITY
   - Liability cap: 2x annual contract value.
   - Neither party liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

3. INDEMNIFICATION
   - Mutual indemnification.
   - Vendor must indemnify Deltek for:
     a) Intellectual Property infringement claims
     b) Data breach or unauthorized disclosure of Deltek data
   - Deltek must indemnify vendor for claims arising from Deltek's misuse of vendor platform.

4. TERMINATION
   - Either party may terminate with 60 days written notice.
   - Immediate termination permitted for material breach (with 30-day cure period).
   - No early termination fees.

5. DATA HANDLING & SECURITY
   - Vendor must maintain SOC 2 Type II certification (current within 12 months).
   - Data residency: United States or European Union only.
   - Vendor must notify Deltek of any data breach within 48 hours.
   - Upon termination, vendor must delete all Deltek data within 30 days and provide written certification.

6. NON-COMPETE
   - No non-compete clauses required.

7. AUTO-RENEWAL
   - Auto-renewal clauses are PROHIBITED.
   - All contract renewals require explicit written consent from both parties.

8. GOVERNING LAW
   - All agreements governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia.
   - Disputes resolved through binding arbitration in Fairfax County, Virginia.
